We're excited to launch Immunospec, a company dedicated to transforming how autoimmune diseases are treated.

## The Problem We're Solving

Over 24 million Americans suffer from autoimmune diseases—conditions where the immune system mistakenly attacks the body's own healthy cells. Current treatments typically rely on broad immunosuppression, which can leave patients vulnerable to infections and other complications.

## Our Approach: Inverse Vaccinology

Instead of suppressing the entire immune system, we're developing therapies that teach it to recognize healthy cells again. Think of it as re-educating your immune system rather than silencing it.
