We're excited to launch Immunospec, because your immune system should be on your side.

## The Problem We're Solving

Over 15 million Americans suffer from autoimmune diseases. These conditions are caused by the immune system mistakenly attacking healthy cells, and current treatments fail to address the root cause. Continuous medication to dampen the immune system can lessen symptoms, but it leaves patients vulnerable to infections, causes side effects, and accumulates significant cost without stopping the underlying autoimmune attack. If we want to prevent, ameliorate, or reverse autoimmune disease, we have to address the root cause.

## Our Approach: Inverse Vaccinology

Patients deserve an option that is: long-lasting, highly precise, and disease-specific. For these reasons, we are pursuing inverse- or reverse-vaccines that can tell the immune system exactly what healthy tissue looks like. Instead of relying on external pharmaceuticals, Immunospec uses natural building blocks like fats, and nucleic acids to leave a long-lasting immune memory: "This signature belongs to a healthy cell. Do not attack it, defend it. Tell the others." This highly specific approach allows us to re-educate the immune system instead of artificially silencing it.
